# Heatwave figure legends (draft)

**Figure 5. The MPA trophic cascade strengthens with the 2014–2016 marine
heatwave in Southern California.** Per-taxon response ratios of inside-MPA to
paired-reference abundance (back-transformed to RR = MPA / Reference; log axis),
estimated as marginal means (± 95% CI) from linear mixed models
`lnRR ~ period + (1 | MPA)`, across the *before* (≤2013), *during* (2014–2016),
and *after* (≥2017) heatwave periods. Taxa are ordered by trophic role: predators
(*Panulirus interruptus*, *Semicossyphus pulcher*), herbivores
(*Strongylocentrotus purpuratus*, *Mesocentrotus franciscanus*), and the producer
(*Macrocystis pyrifera*). Values above the dotted line (RR > 1) indicate higher
abundance inside MPAs. During and after the heatwave, the inside-MPA advantage
rises for spiny lobster, urchins become suppressed, and giant kelp reverses from
lower to markedly higher inside reserves — the signature of MPA-conferred kelp
resilience. California sheephead remain elevated inside reserves but do not change
with the heatwave. n = 415–788 MPA-year observations per taxon across 11–15 MPAs.
(Marine heatwave periods from a Hobday et al. 2016 / `heatwaveR` OISST catalog;
during/after contrasts vs before in `table_heatwave_contrasts.csv`.)

**Figure S. Trophic-cascade regressions across MPA-years**, analogous to
Kumagai et al. (2024, Fig. 7). Density response ratios (lnRR = MPA / Reference) in
Southern California: giant-kelp lnRR declines with sea-urchin lnRR (left) and
sea-urchin lnRR declines with spiny-lobster lnRR (right). Lines are linear
mixed-model fits (± 95% CI) with a site random intercept; points are MPA-year
means; dotted lines mark RR = 1. Full slopes (incl. sheephead, which is
non-significant) in `table_heatwave_cascade_regression.csv`.
